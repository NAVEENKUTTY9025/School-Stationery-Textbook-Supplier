Everything you need to know

Click on any question to reveal the answer.

How do I place an order?

Placing an order is easy! Browse our catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to provide your shipping address and payment details. Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive an email confirmation with your order number.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and purchase orders for schools and businesses. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placing it. Contact our support team immediately with your order number. Once the order has been processed for shipping, changes may not be possible.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within the United States and Canada. International shipping to select countries is coming soon. Sign up for our newsletter to stay updated on international shipping availability.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package on our carrier's website. You can also view your order status in your account dashboard.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in resellable condition. To initiate a return, contact our support team for a return authorization and shipping label.

How do I get a refund?

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll process your refund within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. You'll receive a confirmation email when your refund is processed.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes, we offer exchanges for items of equal value. Contact our support team to arrange an exchange. Please note that shipping fees may apply for exchanges.

Are your Products eco-friendly?

We prioritize sustainability! Many of our stationery items are made from recycled or FSC-certified materials. We also use minimal, recyclable packaging. Look for the "Eco-Friendly" badge on Product pages.

Do you sell textbooks for all grade levels?

Yes! We stock textbooks for kindergarten through college level. Our catalog includes core subjects like Math, Science, English, History, and more. We also carry AP and honors level textbooks.

Are your Products authentic and high-quality?

Absolutely. We source directly from reputable publishers and manufacturers. All Products are guaranteed authentic and meet or exceed quality standards. If you're unsatisfied with a Product, please contact us.

Do you offer bulk discounts for schools?

Yes! We offer special pricing for schools, districts, and educational institutions. Bulk discounts start at orders of 50+ items. Contact our team for a custom quote tailored to your school's needs.

Can schools use purchase orders?

Absolutely! We accept purchase orders from schools, universities, and educational institutions. Contact our sales team to set up a school account and learn about our PO process.

Do you provide sample packs for teachers?

Yes! We offer sample packs for educators who want to test our Products before making a bulk purchase. Reach out to our team to request a sample pack for your classroom or department.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our support team is here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
