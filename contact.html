Frequently asked

Quick answers to the most common questions we get.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express options are available at checkout for 1–2 day delivery.

What's your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Contact support for a return label.

Do you offer bulk discounts for schools?

Yes! We offer special pricing for schools, districts, and educational institutions. Reach out to our team for a custom quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and purchase orders for schools and businesses.
